Full 90-day outlook, all modeled chase cards

Every card the model covers, ranked by how much its price should move over the next 90 days compared with the rest of the market. Each one comes with a range, not just a single number. The ranking does work, but weakly, and a lot of what looks like skill is really old sets behaving like old sets. It measures what happened. It is not a buy list. The Models page measures how much, two different ways.

Technical wording

Every modeled chase card ranked by its predicted market-relative price move over the next 90 days, each with an honest split-conformal 80 percent band. The headline accuracy is real on paper, and it is heavily entangled with the set-age lifecycle: see Models for exactly how much, measured two ways.

    90-day outlook, all modeled cards

    Predicted market-relative return over about 90 days from the snapshot, each with its split-conformal 80 percent band drawn as a bar. The primary flag uses the interval rule (the whole band must clear zero); the secondary chip shows the older top or bottom decile percentile rule. Sort by any header; filter by rarity, set, or flag; or pick a confidence preset. Each card links to its TCGplayer and Cardmarket product pages. A flag is a lead to check, not a call: every card is tested at once, so a few flags fire by chance even when nothing real is there.
