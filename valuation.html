Valuation: what each chase card is worth vs its price

Every modeled chase card priced against a hedonic fair value from its pull cost and desirability, with an honest 80 percent interval and an interval-aware over/under-valued verdict. The verdict describes mispricing versus fundamentals — it is not a return forecast: the pooled undervalued-minus-overvalued forward spread is small and regime-dependent.

Fair value comes from a hedonic regression — log(price) ~ log(pull_cost) + desirability (45/45/10 premium/artwork/appeal), leave-one-out R² 0.628. The residual is how far the market sits from that fair value.

The 80% interval mirrors the forward model’s conformal band; a card is flagged only when its price clears the whole band. Honest coverage (jackknifed) is 79.6%.

This is a mispricing map, not a buy list. The flags describe value versus fundamentals; backtested out of sample the undervalued-minus-overvalued forward spread is only +0.2pt and regime-dependent.

Methodology & honest caveats

The hedonic fair value

Fair value is the point estimate; residual % is how far the market sits from it. A positive residual reads overvalued versus fundamentals, negative undervalued.

The 80% interval & verdict

The verdict is interval-aware: the market must clear the whole band, not just the point, to be flagged. We publish coverage_loo (the honest jackknife over the quantile estimate); the naive in-sample number is circular and labelled as such.

Hierarchical cross-check

Disagreement between the pooled OLS verdict and the per-set Bayesian one is surfaced, not hidden — it flags cards whose set context pulls fair value away from the pooled fit.

Persistence & combined signals

Rank persistence is not return persistence; the combined signals are descriptive pairings of the interval verdict with a recent price move (the momentum study finds no significant short-horizon effect), not a forecast of any single card.

Read this before treating any flag as a buy or sell

  • The flags describe mispricing, not future returns. Backtested at 7 historical snapshots with leakage-free as-of-T features, the pooled undervalued-minus- overvalued forward spread is only about and is regime-dependent (2025 negative, 2026 positive). An over/under-valued flag has NOT predicted returns out of sample.
  • Persistence is descriptive. A card can stay in the overvalued tail for snapshots on end without being shown to underperform; see the spread above.
  • Two price sources by design. The fit uses the live market snapshot; trailing returns use the canonical monthly panel (), so the two deliberately differ.
