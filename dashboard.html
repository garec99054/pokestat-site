pokestat: 90-day chase-card outlook

A walk-forward model ranks every modeled chase card by its predicted market-relative price move over the next 90 days, with an honest uncertainty band on each prediction. The headline accuracy is real on paper; how much is a durable edge versus a set-age lifecycle pattern is stated plainly by the honest verdict and on the Methods page.

This ranks 441 cards by how much their price should move over the next 90 days, compared with the market. Every prediction comes with a range, not just a number. The ranking works, but only weakly, and some of it is just old sets behaving like old sets. It measures what happened. It is not a buy list.
