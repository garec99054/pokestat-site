Compendium: the chase cards of every recent set

Every big-money card from the sets that came out in the last two years, with what it costs now, how its price has moved, its picture, and what two other marketplaces last said it was worth. No forecasts on this page.

Technical wording

Every chase-rarity card from the sets released in the trailing two-year window, with its monthly TCGplayer price panel, trailing 1m/3m/6m moves, full artwork, and a point-in-time cross-check against the Cardmarket and TCGdex snapshots. Prices and realized history only: the newest sets here have no forward model output, so this page carries no predictions.

Recent sets, newest first

Every chase card in the window

Search matches the card name, the set name, the set code and the card number, so me5, Mega, 114 and Charizard all find their rows. Filters and the search box combine; the preset buttons narrow further.

The price tracker and the 1m/3m/6m columns come from one source only: the monthly TCGplayer USD panel assembled from the tcgcsv daily archive. A horizon shows n/a when the card has no observation at exactly that month, which is the normal case for a set released weeks ago. Nothing is filled in from a nearby month.

The last two columns are single snapshots from two other reads, not series. See the cross-source section below for their dates and coverage.

What this page does not do

No predictions, on purpose

Showing the model for the older half of the window only would have made the newest sets look quietly different for a reason no reader could see. Prices and realized history are the same measurement for every card here, so that is all this page carries.

One series, two snapshots

Only the monthly panel is a series. The other two reads hold a handful of dates across their whole tables, so they are shown as a point-in-time comparison with their own as-of date. No line, no arrow, no move over time is drawn from either.

Short panels are short

A trailing return needs an observation at exactly that month. Where there is none the cell says n/a and stays that way; no nearby month is substituted, and no set's first months are extrapolated backwards.

Currency and artwork

Read this before treating any row as a valuation

  • Chase rarity is a catalog fact, not a quality judgement. Membership here is the rarity string the catalog carries, so a set's chase count reflects how that set was printed, not how good its cards are.
  • The panel price is a monthly aggregate. It is the market price the tcgcsv daily archive resolves for that month, not a transaction you could have made, and not a bid or an ask.
  • Cross-source gaps mix two things. A card whose live snapshot differs from its panel price is showing source disagreement AND genuine drift between two different dates. The gap column cannot separate them.
  • Coverage is uneven by set. Several of the newest sets have no rows at all in one or both snapshot tables. Their cells read n/a, and the per-set table above shows exactly where.
  • Nothing here is ranked or recommended. The sort order is whichever column you clicked. No row is flagged, scored or suggested.
