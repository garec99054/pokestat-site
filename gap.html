The Gap: Europe vs US pricing for the same card

Every card this project prices in both Europe and the US, on the same day, converted to one currency so the two numbers can be compared directly. Some cards run dearer in Europe, some run dearer in the US. This page only measures that gap; it does not say what to do about it.

Technical wording

For every card priced in both markets on the newest common date, the FX-adjusted Cardmarket (EUR) price is compared with the TCGplayer (USD) price for the identical card and variant. The ratio is a measurement of where each market prices the same card, not a signal to act on.

Experimental: these rankings come from models that have not passed a forward test yet. Each month's predictions are saved before prices move and scored after three months. Not a recommendation. Any model return shown here is before costs. Bought at a realistic price and sold after fees, the top 10% returned -22.9% over 3 months against -37.3% for all cards. It beat the average card in 7 of 8 months and made money in 0 of 8. Fixed fees and shipping swamp cheap cards: among cards $20 and up, the top 10% returned -3.7% against -15.0%, beat them in 8 of 8 months and made money in 4 of 8. The months overlap, so these are not independent results.

Read this before drawing any conclusion

This is a measurement, not advice. Cardmarket's plain listing is compared with the card's base TCGplayer print, whichever finish that print actually is; its holo listing is compared with that same card's reverse holo print. Shipping, marketplace fees, VAT and card condition are not included on either side. A card with either price under one dollar is flagged penny; a ratio at that scale is noisy. A card observed on only one date is flagged single: one snapshot, not a trend. Whether any card's gap narrows, widens or reverts over time is not yet known from this data.

Exchange rate used: 1 EUR = 1.1130 USD, as of 2026-10-29.

This page carries the 454 modeled chase cards and the 400 widest gaps among the other 7,064 pairs; the complete table for the date is in market_gap.csv.
