Reversion watch

NOT A SIGNAL. Pooled over the whole modeled universe the study measures a 1-month formation spread of -2.9 pts (t_eff -1.82, verdict null) and a 3-month spread of -6.4 pts (t_eff -1.74, verdict null); spread = past winners minus past losers over the next 3 months. Neither is distinguishable from zero once the overlapping 3-month holds are deflated, so the pooled study supports NO tradeable momentum and NO reliable reversion. What follows is simply today's biggest realized movers: there is no measured cross-sectional tendency behind the ordering, and nothing here is a forecast. The only forecast on the row is the model's own predicted 3m. A stratified re-run (see momentum_summary.md) does find reversion inside individual strata, but that is a post-hoc subgroup result with no trading costs applied and it does not license reading this list as a signal.
